The idea

A shed full of easels, a cabin by a creek, five acres of subtropical hinterland and absolutely nothing to do.

Both of these places began life as working art studios. When we converted them we kept what was already good — the original timber, the tin wall behind the wood stove, the three-metre ceilings, the paint on the bench.

What you get is a small, characterful building in a very quiet pocket of the Noosa hinterland: wallabies at dusk, mangoes and bush lemons you're welcome to pick, a fire to light after dark, and the Eumundi Markets ten minutes down the hill. There are easels and charcoal in the cupboard if the mood takes you. Most people just sit on the deck.

The place

A secret pocket
of the hinterland.

Eerwah Vale is the green, folded country behind Eumundi — dairy paddocks, scribbly gums, the odd macadamia grove, and Mount Eerwah standing over all of it. It is close enough to the coast to have sand in the car by lunchtime, and far enough back that you'll hear nothing at night but frogs.

Your hosts

We live at the bottom
of the driveway.

We built both of these studios ourselves, slowly, mostly out of what was already here. We're around if you need us and entirely out of the way if you don't — you'll have your own key, your own deck and your own gate.

Arrive any time after 2pm. Leave by 10am. Somewhere in between, we hope you find the fruit trees.

Shanika

A tame horse who roams the property and will absolutely supervise your arrival. Leave a car space when you open the gate.

The wallabies

Come out across the paddock at dusk and again first thing. Best watched from the north deck with a cup of tea.

Your dog

Generally not a problem — just ask first. It's very good walking and cycling country.
